TITLE
Don't Wake the Giant

SHORT DESCRIPTION (170 characters)
Sneak your grabber into a sleeping giant's treasure pile and steal the weird creatures inside. The deeper you reach, the rarer the loot, but one open eye and he wakes up.

LONG DESCRIPTION (1064 characters)
A giant is fast asleep on a mountain of gold, and the pile is full of strange creatures. Hold to push your grabber into the pile, let go to grab. The deeper you reach, the rarer the creature, and the deepest ones sit right under his chin.

He only stirs at the top of a breath, and he always warns you: the snoring stops, a red exclamation mark pops up, an eyebrow twitches, then the eye cracks open. If your grabber is still in the pile when it opens, he wakes with a roar and swats you into the sky, and half your loot spills. Pull out at the last instant for a close call and double coins. Later in the night he gets restless and starts faking: a snort, a lip smack, a popping snot bubble. Learn to tell a real stir from a fake.

Every creature is two critters stitched together, like Toasty Chompini or Froggo Meowzini: 256 to collect across six rarities, plus Golden, Rainbow, Huge and Tiny ones. Spend your coins on quicker paws, softer socks, bigger ears and a longer arm that opens the vault under his chin. Survive until dawn and the next night pays more.

CONTROLS
Mouse: Press and hold anywhere to reach, release to grab.
Touch: Press and hold anywhere to reach, lift your finger to grab.
Keyboard: Hold Space, Up, W or Enter to reach, release to grab. M to mute, P to pause, B for the creature book.
