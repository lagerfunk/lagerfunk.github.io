TITLE
Seesaw Sumo

SHORT DESCRIPTION (146 characters)
One-button sumo on a seesaw over lava. Hold to charge, jump, belly slam your end down and throw your rival into the sky. Play the CPU or a friend.

LONG DESCRIPTION (1028 characters)
Two jelly sumo blobs stand on a giant seesaw over a pit of lava. You never touch your opponent: the plank is your only weapon. Hold to crouch and charge, let go to jump, tap in the air to belly slam straight down. Land on your end while it is up and the other end whips into the sky, taking your rival with it.

Every jump is a bet. A big slam can send them to orbit, but while you are in the air you are open to their counter-slam. Dodge by hopping at the right moment, save yourself from a bad throw with a mid-air slam, and push your rival toward the edge one throw at a time. First to 3 knockouts wins.

Play the CPU on Easy, Normal or Hard, or grab a friend: 2 players on one keyboard or one touch screen, one button each. Five arenas rotate every round: Volcano, slippery Ice Plank, bouncy Jelly Plank, a Rolling Pivot and Storm Peak with gusts of wind. Win trophies to unlock 12 skins, from a frog to a viking to a king.

Every knockout gets slow motion, a word like YEET or SENT TO ORBIT, and the throw height in metres.

CONTROLS
Mouse: Click and hold to charge, release to jump, click in the air to belly slam.
Touch: Touch and hold to charge, release to jump, tap in the air to slam. In 2 player mode the left half is player 1 and the right half is player 2.
Keyboard (vs CPU): Space, or A, W, S, Q, Z, X, L, K, O, Enter or the arrow keys. Hold, release, tap. P to pause, M to mute.
Keyboard (2 players): Player 1 (red): A. Player 2 (blue): L. One button each.
