TITLE
Fluff Herd.io

SHORT DESCRIPTION (145 characters)
Be a sheepdog. Push fluffy flocks into your pen, bark to scatter your rivals' flocks and steer the stampede home. Your farm earns between rounds.

LONG DESCRIPTION (1276 characters)
You are a sheepdog in a meadow full of fluffy sheep, and so are nine other dogs. Sheep run away from dogs, so you never grab them: you get behind a flock and push it, and the whole flock pours through your gate. The bigger the flock, the bigger the combo.

Bark to scatter every sheep around you. Use it on the flock another dog has spent a minute building, right in front of their gate, or use it to blast your own flock through the last few metres.

Every round brings its own surprises: a Golden Sheep worth 10, a mythic Rainbow Sheep worth 25, a wolf that goes for the biggest flock (bark to chase it off), and at 2:30 the ground shakes. A stampede of 200 sheep follows a bell ram across the meadow. Push the ram into your pen and the whole river follows it in.

Every sheep you pen moves to your farm, and the farm grows wool even while you are away. Spend wool on speed, a bigger bark and a wider pen, unlock ten dog breeds from Collie to Robo-Pup, and watch the farm grow a barn, a windmill, a duck pond and more. Rounds last 3 minutes and the next one starts by itself.

Arena game against bots with their own styles: patient shepherds, gate campers, chaos pups, greedy hoarders and rookies who sometimes push sheep into your pen. No account needed, no online players.

CONTROLS
Mouse: The dog follows the mouse. Click to bark.
Touch: Drag anywhere to run (a stick appears under your thumb). Tap, tap with a second finger, or press BARK to bark.
Keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys to run, Space (or J, Enter) to bark, P or Esc to pause, M to mute.
