TITLE
Grow a Sky Garden

SHORT DESCRIPTION (154 characters)
Be Puff the rain cloud. Rain grows your sky garden, lightning, snow and sunbeams make rare Golden and Rainbow plants. Shoo the crows and grow new islands.

LONG DESCRIPTION (1157 characters)
You are Puff, a little rain cloud floating over a garden in the sky. Drag Puff over the sprouts and the rain makes them bloom with a pop of petals. Each run lasts as long as your water tank, then you spend your petals on the Sky Tree and rain again.

The weather is your toolbox. Tap to zap lightning that chains across your plants and makes them Shocked (x3). Snow turns them Frosted. Keep a sunbeam dry and the plant turns Golden (x5); rain on a sunbeam and you get a Rainbow (x10).

Crows in bandit masks swoop in for your ripest plant. Rain on them to shoo them off, or zap them for a golden feather.

Fill the Seed Book with 32 plants in 5 finishes, from the Daisy Doo to the Mythic Starbloom; every new find gives +1% petals for good. Wake the Giant Bud with rain, lightning, snow and sun in one run, and it blooms into a New Season: a new sky island, bigger petals, new plants. Sprinklers keep growing your garden while you are away, the Daily Gift grows every day you come back, and the Daily Sky is the same challenge garden for every player each day.

Winter Skies (15 Nov to end of February): snow falling, Puff in a beanie, Snowy plants to find.

CONTROLS
Touch: Drag anywhere to move the cloud (the rain lands just above your finger). Quick tap: lightning. SNOW button or two fingers: snow.
Mouse: The cloud follows the mouse. Click: lightning. Hold the right button: snow.
Keyboard: Arrows or WASD to move, Z or J for lightning, hold Space or X to snow, Enter to rain again, M to mute, P or Esc to pause.
