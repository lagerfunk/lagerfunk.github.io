TITLE
The Floor Is Hungry

SHORT DESCRIPTION (147 characters)
The floor woke up hungry and it is coming up to eat you. Tap to jump, wall-jump up candy shafts, rescue snack friends and climb as high as you can.

LONG DESCRIPTION (961 characters)
You are a little dumpling at the bottom of the Snack Tower, and the floor just woke up. It is huge, it is purple, it has teeth, and it is hungry.

Your snack runs by itself. Tap to jump up through the wafers. When you hit a wall in mid-air, tap again to wall-jump and zig-zag up the candy shafts. Bounce on jelly springs, hop over rock-candy spikes, and keep moving on the cookies before they crumble. The floor eats everything below you, and when it gets close its tongue starts to lick the air. Let it catch you and it is GULP, then BURP.

Pop the bubbles to rescue 26 snack friends, each in Golden and Rainbow versions. Every friend you rescue adds coins forever, and you can climb as any of them. Spend coins on Lucky Bubbles, Sticky Socks, a Big Breakfast that slows the floor down, and more.

Climb through five worlds: Candy Kitchen, Gummy Garden, Ice Cream Peaks, Cake Clouds and Sugar Space. Every day there is a new Daily Tower, the same for everyone.

CONTROLS
Mouse: Click anywhere to jump. Click again while touching a wall in mid-air to wall-jump.
Touch: Tap anywhere to jump and wall-jump. Your snack runs left and right by itself.
Keyboard: Space, Up arrow or W to jump. P to pause. M to mute.
