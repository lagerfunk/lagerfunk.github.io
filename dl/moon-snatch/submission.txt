TITLE
Moon Snatch.io

SHORT DESCRIPTION (136 characters)
Be a planet. Grab moons, fling them at rivals and snatch their orbit. Every shot makes you smaller. Biggest planet after 3 minutes wins.

LONG DESCRIPTION (821 characters)
You are a planet with a face, and your moons are everything: your score, your shield and your ammo at the same time.

Fly into loose moons to grow. Fling a moon at a rival and a third of their moons burst out like a pinata for anyone to grab. Big planets knock out small ones in one hit, so small planets fight back by diving through a big planet's orbit and snatching its moons. Every shot you take makes you a little smaller, so choose your moment.

Rounds last 3 minutes against bots with their own styles: hunters, snipers, hoarders, snatchers and pure chaos. Grab the Golden Moon worth 10 moons, survive the Final 30 moon rain and finish the round wearing the crown. Earn coins for planet skins and trails, and keep your daily challenge streak going.

Arena action against bots. No account needed, no online players.

CONTROLS
Mouse: The planet follows the mouse. Click to fling a moon at the highlighted planet, or wherever you click.
Touch: Drag anywhere to steer. Tap to fling. Two fingers work together: steer with one, tap with the other.
Keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys to steer. Space, J or Enter to fling. P to pause, M to mute.
