## Title

Untangle the Lights

## One-line pitch

A box of tangled fairy lights: pull each strand out the way its plug points, in the right order, and light up a snowy town.

## Short description (about 160 characters)

Tap a strand of fairy lights to pull it out the way its plug points. Free them all without a bump, light up the town and collect new light sets.

## Long description

Someone packed the fairy lights in a hurry, and now the box is one big tangle. Tap a strand and it slides out the way its plug points, lights up and flies off to hang over the town. But a strand only gets out if nothing is in its way. Tap one that is blocked and it bumps, and every bump blows a fuse.

Find the strands that are free, pull them in quick succession for combos, and watch the tangle open up. Every board can be solved, and there is never a wrong order: only bumps cost you.

New things keep turning up: presents that never move, frozen strands that thaw when a neighbour leaves, padlocked plugs that open when you free the strand carrying the key, and golden strands that pay you a hint.

Each level lights a window in the town, ten windows light a street. Clear levels without a bump for 3 stars, and stars unlock new light sets: Candy Cane, Icicle, Aurora, Neon Party, Golden and Rainbow (plus a Pumpkin set during Halloween).

Every day there is one Daily board, the same for every player, with a streak and a result line to share. Marathon mode deals board after board until your fuses run out. Stuck? A hint lights up a strand that can go.

## How to play

1. Tap a strand. It slides out in the direction its plug points.
2. If another strand or a present is in the way, it bumps and you lose a fuse. Lose all your fuses and the lights go out.
3. Frozen strands thaw when a strand next to them is pulled out. A padlocked strand opens when the strand with its key is pulled out.
4. Clear the box. No bumps = 3 stars.

## Controls

- Touch or mouse: tap or click a strand to pull it. Tap the bulb button for a hint.
- Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to pick a strand, Space or Enter to pull it, H for a hint, M to mute, P or Esc for the menu.

## Questionnaire answers

- Multiplayer: no.
- Orientation: works in portrait and landscape; portrait first on phones.
- Mobile and tablet: yes, touch controls built in.
- Languages: English.
- Saves: progress saved in the browser (CrazyGames: Data module).
- Ads: `gameplayStart` on the first input and whenever play resumes, `gameplayStop` on every result card, menu, ad and hidden tab. `requestAd('midgame')` only between boards (after a level card, a Marathon board or a retry), never in the first 3 minutes of play or before level 5, at most once every 3 minutes, never within 10 seconds of a rewarded ad and never on the player's first three lights-outs. `requestAd('rewarded')` is always optional: +2 hints (at most 5 a day) or +2 fuses after a lights-out (once per board, once per Marathon run). The reward is paid on `adFinished`. During Basic Launch the SDK answers `adsDisabledBasicLaunch`: the game then pays the reward anyway and the offers say Free.
- Pause: the game and its sound stop when the tab is hidden and resume when it comes back. The CrazyGames sound button is followed.
- Age rating: PEGI 3 style. No violence, no fear, no text input, no chat, no data collection, no purchases.
- Original work: name, rules presentation, art, music and sound are original and made in code. Fonts: Lilita One and Fredoka (SIL Open Font License, licences inside the zip).
