TITLE
Steal a Plane

SHORT DESCRIPTION (147 characters)
Steal planes from rival airfields, take off from their runway with one perfect tap and dodge the guard goose. Your hangar earns coins every second.

LONG DESCRIPTION (1037 characters)
Your airfield earns coins every second from the planes parked in its hangar, and the rarer the plane, the more it earns. Buy planes as they roll down the Plane Market, or take the fast way: steal them.

Three rival pilots run the airfields next door. When their gate laser switches off, sneak in, grab a plane and get out. Taxi it home, or try the takeoff heist: roll down the rival's own runway and tap when the needle hits the green. A perfect takeoff pays double and can paint your plane gold.

Watch out for the guard goose in the hi-vis vest. When it is awake it will chase you down and honk you right out of the field. And while you are away, the rivals come for your planes, so race home and tackle the thief before they reach their gate.

Collect 20 planes from a paper plane to a golden jumbo and a flying saucer, in Gold, Diamond and Rainbow paint jobs. Fill the Plane Book for bonus income, upgrade your gate lock, sneakers and goose, and rebirth for a permanent income multiplier. Your fleet keeps earning while you are away.

CONTROLS
Mouse: Drag to move, or click the ground to walk there. Click market planes to buy. Click during a takeoff.
Touch: Drag anywhere to move (floating joystick). Tap a plane on the market to buy it. During a takeoff, tap anywhere.
Keyboard: WASD or the arrow keys to move. Space or Enter to take off. P to pause, M to mute.
