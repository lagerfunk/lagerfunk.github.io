TITLE
Hangar Merge

SHORT DESCRIPTION (144 characters)
Drop planes into the hangar, merge matching pairs into bigger jets, then launch the big ones for a bonus that grows the fuller your hangar gets.

LONG DESCRIPTION (894 characters)
Hangar Merge is a merge puzzle with a twist: the big planes take off.

Drop paper planes, gliders and crop dusters into the hangar. Two of the same merge into the next plane up, all the way to the Jumbo, and three in one go skip a size. Every plane has a face: they blink, cheer when they are ready to fly and panic when the hangar is nearly full.

From the Bizjet up, a plane glows when it is ready. Tap it and it climbs out of the hangar, freeing its space and paying a bonus. The fuller the hangar, the bigger the payout, so every takeoff is a bet: launch now to make room, or wait for a bigger multiplier. Tower requests pay double, two Jumbos leave together in formation, and chains of merges multiply your score.

Coins from every flight build your airport. Collect all 10 planes and their golden twins, unlock paint jobs, and play the Daily Flight: the same planes for everyone each day.

CONTROLS
Mouse: Move to aim, click to drop. Click a glowing plane to take off.
Touch: Drag to aim, let go to drop. Tap a glowing plane to take off.
Keyboard: Left and Right arrows or A and D to aim. Space, Down, S or Enter to drop. Up, W or T to launch the biggest ready plane. Q to swap. P for the menu. M to mute.
