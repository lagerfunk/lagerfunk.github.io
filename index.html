Games

Six HTML5 games. Mobile and desktop, no install.

  • Hangar MergeDrop and merge planes into bigger jets, then launch them for bonus points.
  • Steal a PlaneSneak into rival airfields past the guard goose and fly off with their best planes.
  • The Floor Is HungryYou are a snack and the floor is rising to eat you. One button, climb the candy tower.
  • Moon Snatch.ioYou are a planet. Grab moons, fling them at rivals and finish the round on top.
  • Don't Wake the GiantReach into a sleeping giant's treasure pile and let go before it wakes.
  • Seesaw SumoTwo jelly sumo blobs on a seesaw over lava. Slam your end to launch your rival.
